Same engine, at seller inspection (described here, not built)

Unpaid challans now block car transactions. On 28 Sep 2026 the Supreme Court directed states to blacklist vehicles with unpaid e-challans and block ownership transfer, and a draft amendment would add a “Not to be Transacted” flag (not yet in force). Every challan on a car Cars24 buys therefore becomes a holdback or a delay in the RC transfer.

Run the same state machine at inspection:

  1. Fetch the car's challans.
  2. Pay online and virtual-court challans directly.
  3. Route physical-court challans to a lawyer, with the timeline disclosed.
  4. Release the seller's holdback only on government confirmation, and refund automatically if a deadline is missed.

The seller hands over a car with a challan status that has been checked, and Cars24 holds back money only for challans that are actually open.

Method

  1. Collect. 1-2★ Google Play reviews of Cars24, CarInfo and VehicleInfo, 2025-01-01 to 2026-10-22, pulled on 2026-10-22. The SHA-256 of each raw file is in data/metrics.json.
  2. Mask. Reviewer names and photos are never shipped. Phone numbers, emails, registration numbers, challan numbers, UPI handles and ID numbers are masked, and a test fails the build if any leak.
  3. Classify. Deterministic keyword rules covering English, Hinglish and Devanagari, with no language model. Every build gives the same result.
  4. Label. A stratified random sample of 150 challan reviews (seed 24) was read one by one and labelled against the six definitions. The rules were tuned on rows 1 to 50; rows 51 to 150 were held out and scored once. Labels and rules come from the same analysis, so the held-out score is probably a little optimistic.
  5. Estimate. Headline shares come from the labelled sample with 95% Wilson intervals, scaled to all challan reviews.

What this does not show

  • Reviews are self-selected, so these are complaint counts, not failure rates.
  • Whether a stuck challan is Cars24's fault or its partner's is inferred from how the user describes it, not from internal records.
  • No live Parivahan or e-challan calls and no Cars24 data. The engine's government confirmation is a stub.
  • No regulator action against any challan app was found as of 2026-10-22.
